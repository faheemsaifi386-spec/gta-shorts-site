Privacy Policy

GTA 6 Daily is a personal tool used only by its owner. It requests permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

The app does not collect, sell, share or store personal data of any other users. The authorization token is stored privately by the owner and is used only to upload videos.

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
